My depiction of my street

Context:

During the first year at CMD I was asked to make two visualizations of my street and how I look at it. I found out during the first year that I like to draw stuff by hand and digitalize it. With this, 3D and digitalizing materials I made two posters that show my positive and negative view of the street. On the one hand I saw a beautiful street with a lot of friendly cats which I centralized in the positve poster. On the other hand I wanted to show that the street also had noise problems mostly from cars which I visualized with a noisy car. In the center I made an icon from food which resembles feces and that there was also polution by animals in the street.

In the end I was happy with what I could accomplish without a lot of prior knowledge of designing posters, and that I could find my own way to make something. While I saw many other posters that went for a minimalistic and sleek design, I wanted too try the opposite of it.

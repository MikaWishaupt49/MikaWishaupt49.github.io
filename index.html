EduCAD

An adaptive education tool to accelerate the learning process of inCAD for different employees

  • RoleDesigner & Researcher
  • Year2026
  • Tools examplesGodot, Inkscape , LM Studio

About the project

For Nunu (see link) I worked on their tool called inCAD to let people learn more effectively based on their learning outcomes. link inCAD.

In the end I came up with EduCAD, an additional tool that leverages the power of AI and data to accelerate the learning process of inCAD. Different users come with different needs, inCAD comes with different tasks from the perspective of an electrical engineer to a project manager. EduCAD let's users create adaptive learning courses based on their wishes and needs. A project might need further education in file structuring while an engineer needs deeper understanding of the schematics. With eduCAD, both can learn on their own pace with the help of AI that can help you with difficult tasks.

Not only did eduCAD gave me ideas how to make more accessible education for different users, it also showed me the important ethical decisions when using with personal AI and data. With a potential futurue with more implementation of AI and data, it becomes more and more important to have designers also working as researchers and work towards the best outcome for different stakeholders.

Important highlights

  • Ethical understanding of AI and data
  • Working with emerging technology
  • Deep research from different academic sources and working on my own insights

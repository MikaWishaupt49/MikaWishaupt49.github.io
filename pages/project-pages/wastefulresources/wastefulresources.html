The future to create

Context:
Wasteful Resources is a made concept that shows the dangers of AI for the creative industry in videogames. When I started with WR I had no idea I would make a videogame at the end of the journey. I started by a major research around the topic of Planned Obsolescence. I saw the waste of recourcess from mobiles intentionally getting worse to clothes so that we keep consuming more and companies can profit more. After researh I looked more abstract to Planned Obsolescence and started seeing humans as a sort of resource that could be exploited and replaced so that companies can profit more and more. This and the combination of the fear of AI taking over jobs made me made Wasterful Resources.

The project:
The project contains 2 sides: The physical side: The physical side shows a wall that seperates two world. One world with a game made by people and the other a game made by AI. The people side contains a game that is made by me and participants that showed their creative side which I made in different levels for a human game that shows people the fun and creativity that comes with making games. The other side is an AI game which I made with research how AI could visualize a game using principles like Generative Design. While people play the AI game, an office building representing a creative gaming office is getting drowned by the AI to show the fast growth that AI has and the bad influence it can have on certain workers. The digital side: The digital side just like the physical sides contain two games which show a human made game and an AI game.
